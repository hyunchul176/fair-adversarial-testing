# Design-decision ledger

This document holds the design-decision ledger (former Section S8, Table S11). Its text comes verbatim from the supplementary material of the paper "Fair adversarial testing of automated vehicles in highway cut-ins: reference-shielded attacks and a capability-controlled evaluation", which is kept in this repository instead of being published with the paper (supplement draft v13 of 2026-10-12, text as of 2026-11-14). Section, figure and table numbers without "S" refer to the paper; numbers with "S" refer to sections and tables of these documents, and README.md lists where each is. The figures of the supplementary material are not reproduced.

Table S11 compiles the design decisions from the decision log of the project, the run registry and the design documents (read-only check, 2026-10-04). Dev-62 denotes the 62 development cases from three highD recordings (in-sample). B9-148 denotes the reserved highD cases, used once in a pre-specified evaluation on 2026-09-25 and reused post hoc afterwards; under the project's own rule of 2026-09-25 such reuse counts as development data. Ladder denotes the seven rule-based SUTs of the training panel.

Some uncertainties remain. Most decisions are logged by date only, and the 2026-10-01 switch to v2s has no recorded time (file metadata place the new exiD draft after the post-hoc MiTra log entry). No rationale is recorded for the 1 IQR envelope or the 0.75 M-step warm-start checkpoint. The ledger leaves out implementation fixes and side studies, so it lists fewer entries than the roughly sixty design iterations.
